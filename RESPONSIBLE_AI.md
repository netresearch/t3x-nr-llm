# Responsible AI

nr-llm provides shared controls for AI applications in TYPO3. The
[Responsible AI documentation](Documentation/ResponsibleAi/Index.rst)
records available capabilities, limitations, concrete gaps, responsibilities
and a comparison with external frameworks. It distinguishes source/test
evidence from evidence about a deployed application.

The extension is not a certification of an application, operator or provider.
Settings, benchmarks and TYPO3 core recognition do not establish that status.
See [ADR-269](Documentation/Adr/Adr269ResponsibleAiClaimsRequireEvidenceAndAnOwner.rst)
for the maintenance decision.

The software remains **GPL-2.0-or-later**. These engineering commitments and
recommendations add no restrictions on use, modification or redistribution.
Descriptions of unsupported or unevaluated uses do not change the licence.

Report ordinary defects through
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/netresearch/t3x-nr-llm/issues).
Report vulnerabilities through
[private security reporting](https://github.com/netresearch/t3x-nr-llm/security/advisories/new).
Do not include confidential data or credentials in public reports.
