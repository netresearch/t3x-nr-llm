- `set_page_social_image` with `replace` removes a copy of the old reference that a translation left on the default-language page, also on TYPO3 14.3.10. Since 14.3.10 the DataHandler aligns every inline child of a page that gets a new one to the page's language and clears its translation pointer (core 68242812b9), so the copy was no longer deleted with its parent; the tool refused the write and reported a second live reference. The tool now names the copy in its delete.
