- A task carries a list of placements, the places where it is offered first. The editors' module *AI tasks* lists the tasks marked for it first under *Prepared for this module* and the tasks marked for no place under their category's label instead of the raw category key; a task marked for other places only is not listed there. `TaskPlacementItems::groupByPlacement()` orders a TCA select of tasks by a placement another extension declares, and shows or hides the unmarked tasks (ADR-245).
