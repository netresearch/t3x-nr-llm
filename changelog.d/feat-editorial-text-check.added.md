- A text for use outside TYPO3 can be checked against the rules of a configuration with the new internal `ExternalTextCheck` service: forbidden and required terms and the rule-set judge apply at every length and to the strings of a JSON text, and the report states `passed`, `violation`, `not_checked` or `failed` together with the digest of the checked text and of the rule state. A text no rule applies to is reported as not checked. The trust-zone rule of the rule-set judge now lives in one class shared by the guardrail and the new service (ADR-300).
