- **Choosing a model in the model form no longer unticks capabilities nobody denied.** "Fetch Models" ticked exactly the capabilities discovery reported and unticked the rest. For a model the built-in catalogue does not list, discovery cannot say what the model lacks: an OpenAI or Gemini model the catalogue does not list yet reports `chat` (Gemini also `streaming`) but nothing about tools or vision, every Groq model reports `chat` alone, and so does an Ollama host that reports no capabilities. Re-picking such a model, or moving a record to the next version of one, silently removed `tools` and `vision` from a record that had them, and readers of the record then treated a tool-calling model as one without tools. Discovery now marks such a list as inferred. When the record already ticks everything the inferred list names, the form keeps its other ticks; otherwise, and for any list the provider or the catalogue states, the list replaces the ticks as before.
