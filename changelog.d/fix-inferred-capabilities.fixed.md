- **Choosing a model in the model form no longer unticks capabilities nobody denied.** "Fetch Models" ticked exactly the capabilities discovery reported and unticked the rest. For some models discovery cannot say what the model lacks: an OpenAI or Gemini chat model the built-in catalogue does not list yet (the listing names `chat`, for Gemini sometimes `streaming`, and never tools or vision), every Groq model (`chat` only), and an Ollama model whose capabilities the host did not report (`chat` only). Re-picking such a model, or moving a record to the next version of one, silently removed `tools` and `vision` from a record that had them, and readers of the record then treated a tool-calling model as one without tools. Discovery now marks such a list as inferred. When the record already ticks the kind of model the inferred list names (chat, embeddings, ...), the form keeps its other ticks; otherwise, and for any list the provider or the catalogue states, the list replaces the ticks as before.
