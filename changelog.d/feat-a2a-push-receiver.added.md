- A2A push notifications in the client role (spec 051 stage 5b, A2A-PUSH6 to A2A-PUSH8, ADR-285). While the new extension setting `a2a.client.push` is on and `a2a.client.pushSite` names a site with an A2A base URL, a delegation to a remote agent whose card declares `pushNotifications` registers a callback with `CreateTaskPushNotificationConfig`: the URL `<base>/push` and a random callback credential as `Authorization: Bearer`, stored as prefix and SHA-256 hash only. After the registration the task is read once with `GetTask`. The endpoint accepts a plain `StreamResponse` without `A2A-Version`; a missing or wrong credential is 401 and stores nothing, `X-A2A-Notification-Token` is ignored, a body above `a2a.client.maxPushBytes` is 413, and the inbox is bounded per delegation (`a2a.client.maxPushInboxPerTask`) and in total (`a2a.client.maxPushInbox`), 429 over a bound. A stored callback, sealed, only brings the next `GetTask` of `nrllm:a2a:poll` forward, never closer than the record's minimum poll interval after the last look; `GetTask` as the run's owner decides the state, and the look deletes the callbacks received until it began, so the inbox bounds also bound what is stored. A create with an unknown outcome is never sent again.
