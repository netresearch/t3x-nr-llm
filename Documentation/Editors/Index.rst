===========
For editors
===========

Use prepared AI tasks to draft text, review suggestions and approve permitted
writes. An administrator first grants access and assigns configurations and
tools; the available actions depend on your permissions.

1. Open :guilabel:`AI > AI Tasks` and choose a prepared task, or start an
   available action beside a record. See :ref:`administration-tasks`.
2. Check the input and submit the task. Your current workspace and record
   permissions still apply.
3. Review the answer and any proposed write. A proposal does not itself save
   the change. Approval is available only where the tool and your grants
   permit it; see :ref:`administration-agent-runs`.
4. Check the resulting record. A partial write needs attention before you
   continue. Workspace writes and publication are separate operations;
   see :ref:`tool-workspaces`.

If an administrator allowed it for your group, you can also create tokens
that let an agent of your own act as you, in :guilabel:`AI > A2A tokens`; see
:ref:`administration-a2a-tokens-editor`. A token replaces your login for
that agent: keep it secret.

Ask your administrator if a task or tool is missing. Access is described in
:ref:`administration-permissions`; approved reusable instructions are covered
in :ref:`administration-skills`.
